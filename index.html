POD IQ

A desktop app for print-on-demand sellers, by Pressed Daily Threads.

POD IQ brings the print-on-demand workflow into one Mac app: finding ideas, making designs, preparing print files, building mockups, and drafting listings.

What it connects to

POD IQ can connect to services you already use, only when you choose to connect them: Etsy, Printify, Pinterest, Canva, EverBee and AI image and text providers. Each connection uses your own account.

Pinterest

When you connect Pinterest, POD IQ can read your own boards, create boards, and publish Pins of your own products to your own account. It does not read other people's accounts.
