POD IQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

POD IQ is a desktop app for print-on-demand sellers, made by Pressed Daily Threads ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • POD IQ runs on your computer. Your designs, settings and account connections are stored on your computer.
  • We do not run servers that collect your data. We do not sell or share your information.
  • The app talks to other services only when you connect them and ask it to do something.

Information stored on your computer

POD IQ stores the following locally on your device: your designs and images, mockups, listing drafts, app settings, and the keys or access tokens for the services you connect. This information stays on your device unless you choose to back it up or send it to a connected service. Backups made by the app leave out keys and tokens.

Services you connect

You can connect POD IQ to third-party services using your own accounts: Etsy, Printify, Pinterest, Canva, EverBee, and AI providers such as OpenAI or OpenRouter. When you use a feature that needs one of them, the app sends that service only what the feature requires (for example, an image and a title when you publish a Pin, or a prompt when you generate an image). Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Pinterest

If you connect your Pinterest account, POD IQ uses Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth). The app never sees or stores your Pinterest password. With your permission, POD IQ can:

  • read the list of your own boards,
  • create boards on your account, and
  • create Pins on your account from images and text you prepared in the app.

POD IQ does not read other people's Pins or boards. The Pinterest access token is stored only on your computer and is used only to carry out actions you start. You can disconnect at any time in POD IQ's settings, or remove the app's access in your Pinterest account's security settings. Disconnecting deletes the stored token from your computer.

Analytics and tracking

POD IQ has no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no analytics that send your activity to us.

Updates

When you check for updates, the app downloads the latest version from our code host. That request does not include your personal information beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address.

Children

POD IQ is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your computer, you can delete it at any time by removing it in the app or uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder. To remove data held by a connected service, use that service's own tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message us through the Pressed Daily Threads shop on Etsy.
